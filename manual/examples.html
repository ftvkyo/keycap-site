The package’s examples/ directory holds complete models:

spacer.fidget
a round standoff for an M3 screw, in fidget’s own dialect, so that fidget’s viewer and CLI run it unchanged.
ivar-joiner.keycap
a plate that joins two IKEA IVAR legs: a sloped edge written as a plane equation, wood screw holes, grid_array and symmetric_y.
table-cable-hook.keycap
a cable tray under a table’s edge: union_round fillets, a cutter array, draw_rgb and reference geometry.
menger-sponge.keycap
a Menger sponge three levels deep: one cross of bars per level, repeated without end and cut from the cube, so the cost grows with the levels and not with the 8000 cubes.
fan-grill.keycap
a 2D drawing of a fan grill: a honeycomb from hex_grid, best seen in the sdf view.
skadis-towel-hook.keycap
a hook for an IKEA SKÅDIS pegboard, importing the board and its peg from skadis.rhai.
knob.keycap
the handle of a knob, with a waist and a dished top: a revolved profile cut with ellipses, its cusps opened with smooth_difference, and a ring of ellipsoid bumps tilted with the dish.
hex-bowl.keycap
a hexagonal bowl pierced by a honeycomb: a rounded prism from offset, a hollow revolved from points::bezier, holes from hex_grid.
badge.keycap
a pin badge and its strap bracket: artwork drawn as strokes, imported with svg and turned into printable lines with offset.
vase-interpolate.keycap
a vase whose outline turns from hexagon to hexagram, back, and into a circle, swaying and widening as it rises: star with a keyframes inner radius, lerp of fields, loft and a shell of even thickness.
vase-koch.keycap
a vase on a Koch snowflake, widening and turning: a fold by mirrors at each level of the fractal, loft.
gear-involute.keycap
an involute spur gear, from module, teeth, clearance and pressure angle, both flanks of every tooth exact; HELIX makes it a herringbone.
dna-loop.keycap
a double helix wound round a ring, with rungs: helix, bend, and one rung repeated and turned with the strands.
feather.keycap
a feather the length of a bed’s diagonal, with two hundred barbs of Bézier curves whose control points run along Bézier curves: bezier_stroke with a tapering width, the stem’s ends cut square with intersection_round, bezier_point.
bed-washers.keycap
bed-levelling washers, 0.5 to 1.0 mm thick, their outlines’ sides telling the thickness: a row by arrange_row, repeated by repeat_grid.
scraper.keycap
a bed scraper with scallops for the fingers and a label: an outline from points::difference of circles, its corners rounded by rounded_polygon, text.
phone-stand.keycap
a phone stand with every edge rounded: OpenSCAD’s minkowski sum with a sphere, exactly, as prisms grown by offset and extruded with rounded rims.
laptop-stand.keycap
two laser-cut legs crossed on printed hinges, and a lock: rounded_polygon outlines with arcs, the 2D parts laid out on keycap/lasercut’s sheet for keycap-export-2d.
laptop-stand-2.keycap
the laptop stand’s second version, its hinges’ arms rounded: a profile from points::union and points::intersection, opened exactly by rounded_polygon.
vase-funky-2.keycap
a vase of hexagons on hexagons, twisting and widening along Bézier schedules: union_round of 43 opened hexagons, a twist from bezier_integral.
vase-practical.keycap
a vase on a hexagram rounded both ways, turning a sixth as it rises: star with rounded points, loft.
whistle.keycap
a whistle with its sizes printed on top, and a slider that tunes it: text from numbers, stroke and union_round, a part to slice across Z.
fidget-magnetic.keycap
a magnetic fidget spinner on a printed bearing: bearing.rhai imported as a module, difference_round, magnets from keycap/magnets.
fidget-flower.keycap
twelve spiral petals on cogs round a housing, on the same bearing: loft through a petal’s slices, repeat_polar.
allen-key-handle.keycap
a T-handle for a bent Allen key: one sweep for the bar, its bends and the stem, its tips tapered by sweep_length, mirrored with symmetric_y.
spike.keycap
a stud that screws through fabric, and the drivers that hold it: a twisted-ellipse thread, revolve from a profile’s points.
pointer.keycap
a pointing hand of square prisms and octahedra, in halves to print: elongate of an octahedron.
bendy-sheet.keycap
a plywood sheet that bends into a half pipe, and its supports: keycap/lasercut’s living_hinge and cross_lap.
intake-manifold.keycap
four runners merging into a collector, hollow: tubes along 3D Bézier curves from points::bezier and capsule, blended with smooth_union, then a shell.
bar-hook.keycap
an S-hook for a round or square bar, drawn flat as a pen would: arc_stroke and stroke, extruded with rounded rims.
desk-cable-clip.keycap
a clamp for a desk’s edge with a ring a cable snaps into, drawn as its side: arc_stroke, stroke and union_round.
command-strip-hook.keycap
a hook on a back sized for an adhesive strip: a bezier_stroke hook filleted into the back with union_round.
zip-tie-mount.keycap
a base with a bridge for a cable tie, screwed or stuck down: a polygon gable for the tunnel, screw_hole countersunk.
