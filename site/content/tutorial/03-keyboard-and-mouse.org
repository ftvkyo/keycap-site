#+title: Keyboard and mouse
#+description: The knob again, worked with the keyboard alone and with a hand on the mouse, and every action with its key and its gesture.

Everything in keycap has a key, and most things can be done with the mouse too.
This part takes the knob from the last two parts and goes through both ways of working, then lists every action with its key and its gesture.
Nothing new gets modelled here.

* Two ways to work

With the keyboard alone, the script's window stays selected while you type, and the preview follows on its own.
The preview's commands that are needed from the script have =C-c= keys: =C-c C-c= shows it, =C-c C-v= switches its view.
For the rest, =C-x o= moves to the preview, where single keys turn the model, switch views and move the slice, and =C-x o= moves back.
There, =?= lists the most used keys in the echo area, and in a 2D view, the slice's keys.
The preview also shows the list by itself, the first time in a session that it shows a 3D view, and the first time it shows a 2D one.

With a hand on the mouse, the keyboard stays in the script and the mouse works on the preview.
Dragging, the wheel and the header line's buttons leave the keyboard where it is, so you can keep typing while the model turns.
A click on the preview selects it, for when you want its keys after all.

* The header line

The words at the start of the preview's header line say what it shows, and each of them is a button.
Clicking =iso= opens the cameras:

#+begin_src keycap :model knob :after "02-fasteners.org" :view shaded :menu camera :alt The camera menu open under the word iso in the preview's header line: front, right, top, isometric, perspective, reset camera, each with its key
#+end_src

- the view, =shaded= :: the four views, with their keys.
- the camera, =iso= :: front, right, top and isometric, perspective, and a reset; once the model is turned by hand, the word is its angles, e.g. =-100° 42°=.
- in a 2D view, the axis and the offset, e.g. =y = 15= :: the axis to slice across; a click on the offset asks for a coordinate, and the wheel over it moves the slice.
  A tilted slice shows its angles too, e.g. =z ⊥ (30°, 0°) at 15=, and a click on them levels it.
- the bounds and the backend :: switch between the script's bounds and fitted ones, and between the GPU and the CPU.
- an error or an export :: a click on an error goes to its line in the script, and one on =Exporting= cancels it.

Hovering a button highlights it and shows a tooltip with what a click does.

* The menus

Right-click the preview for its menu: the views, the cameras (or, in 2D, the slices), the toggles and the exports, each with its key.

#+begin_src keycap :model knob :view shaded :pointer "0.4 0.28" :menu context :alt The preview's context menu open at the mouse, over the knob: go to shape, the four views, the cameras, the toggles and the exports
#+end_src

On the image, the menu starts with "Go to Shape": it moves to the script, at the =draw= of the shape under the mouse.
Double-clicking a shape does the same, which helps when a script draws several shapes and you want to know which line made which.

The same menu is in the menu bar as =Preview=, and the script has its own, =Keycap=, with the script's commands and their keys.
With the menu bar hidden, =F10= opens it.
With =context-menu-mode= on, right-clicking a name in the script offers its documentation, as =C-c C-d= does.

* Scrubbing a slice

In a 2D view, holding Shift turns the wheel into a slice control: =S-wheel= moves the slice a step, the same step as =[= and =]=, and =M-wheel= moves it ten.
=S-drag= scrubs through the model: dragging up moves the slice forward, and the window's height covers the whole of the bounds.
The frames are rendered at half resolution while you drag, and the header line shows where the slice is.

Click the view's word, pick "SDF", then click the axis letter for the slices:

#+begin_src keycap :model knob :slice y :menu slice :alt The slice menu open under the axis letter in the header line: across X, Y and Z, isometric, forward, back, at an offset, level, reset camera
#+end_src

Pick "Across Z", then scrub up from the bottom: the nut's hexagon, then the round hole for the bolt, then the knob narrowing at its waist and the ring of bumps in the dish.
It is the quickest way to find the thinnest wall in a part.

* Tilting a slice

A slice cuts a sloping wall slantwise, and the wall looks thicker on it than it is.
Tilt the slice until it cuts the wall square: =}= and ={= turn it 5° about the image's right, =M-}= and =M-{= about its up, and =C-drag= turns it as if you held it, dragging down bringing its top towards you.
A numeric prefix sets the angle, =M-3 M-0 }= for 30°.

#+begin_src keycap :model knob :slice "z 15 30 0" :alt The slice across Z tilted 30° about the image's right: an oval cut through the knob, the header reading z ⊥ (30°, 0°) at 15
#+end_src

The slice turns about the middle of the bounds, keeps its scale, and the offset runs along its tilted normal; the point under the mouse is read in the model's own coordinates.
=r= levels it where it is, =1=, =3= and =7= slice level across Y, X and Z, and =0= slices through the middle facing the isometric camera, as =0= looks from it in 3D.

* Scrubbing a number

The same gestures work on numbers in the script.
With the mouse over a digit, =S-wheel= adds or subtracts one step of that digit, and the preview follows; over the =2= of =12= the step is 1, over the =1= it is 10.
=S-drag= from a digit changes it by a step per column the mouse moves, right to add and left to subtract, and the whole drag is undone with one =C-/=.

#+begin_src keycap :model knob :at "BUMPS = 12|;" :wheel 4 :alt The mouse over the number of bumps after four turns of the shifted wheel: 16 bumps in the preview, the echo area showing the change
#+end_src

Away from a number, the shifted wheel scrolls sideways and the shifted click opens the font menu, as they do in any buffer.

* Every action

| Action                       | Key                              | Mouse                                                    |
|------------------------------+----------------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------|
| Show the preview             | =C-c C-c=                        | =Keycap= menu                                            |
| Next view                    | =C-c C-v=, =v= in the preview    | header: the view's word                                  |
| One view                     | =s=, =z=, =d=, =m=               | header: the view's word; right-click                     |
| Turn the model               | arrows                           | drag                                                     |
| Move the camera              | =M-= arrows; arrows in 2D        | middle drag; drag in 2D                                  |
| Zoom                         | =+=, =-=                         | wheel                                                    |
| Front, right, top, iso       | =1=, =3=, =7=, =0=               | header: the camera's word; right-click                   |
| Reset the camera and tilt    | =r=                              | header: the camera's word; right-click                   |
| Perspective                  | =p=                              | header: the camera's word; right-click                   |
| Slice across X, Y, Z (2D)    | =3=, =1=, =7=                    | header: the axis letter; right-click                     |
| Slice facing iso (2D)        | =0=                              | header: the axis letter; right-click                     |
| Move the slice (2D)          | =[=, =]=                         | =S-wheel=, =M-wheel= for ten steps, =S-drag=             |
| Put the slice somewhere (2D) | =M-3 ]=, =C-u ]=                 | header: click the offset, or the wheel over it           |
| Tilt the slice (2D)          | ={=, =}=, =M-{=, =M-}=           | =C-drag=; header: click the angles to level it           |
| Name the most used keys      | =?=                              | right-click                                              |
| Read the model               | --                               | hover: the point and the distance in the header          |
| Go to a shape's =draw=       | --                               | double-click the shape; right-click                      |
| Nudge a number               | =C-c C-n=, =C-c C-p=             | =S-wheel= over a digit                                   |
| Scrub a number               | --                               | =S-drag= from a digit                                    |
| Bounds, backend              | =b=, =G=                         | header: their words                                      |
| Describe a name              | =C-c C-d=                        | right-click it, with =context-menu-mode=                 |
| Evaluate an expression       | =C-x C-e=                        | =Keycap= menu                                            |
| Export an STL, a 2D drawing  | =C-c C-e=, =C-c C-l=; =e=, =l=   | =Keycap= menu; right-click the preview                   |
| Cancel an export             | =C-c C-k=; =C-g=                 | header: =Exporting=                                      |
| Go to an error               | =M-x flymake-goto-next-error=    | header: the error                                        |

The single keys are the preview's; the =C-c= keys work in the script.
