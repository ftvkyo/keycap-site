#+title: Tutorial
#+description: Learning keycap by building its examples, step by step.

#+review: reviewed 2026-11-17

The tutorial has nine parts.
Most of them start from an empty file and build a part step by step; parts 2, 3 and 8 go on with the knob from part 1.
The first eight together take an hour or two; the last one is a longer project.
You need keycap [[file:../install.html][installed]] and nothing else: no knowledge of distance fields, and only a little programming.

1. [[file:01-first-model.org][A first model]] -- a knob from a 2D profile: the preview, the distance view, rounding by blending, nudging numbers.
2. [[file:02-fasteners.org][Bumps and a captive nut]] -- finishing the knob: cutters with print clearances, documentation at point, slicing.
3. [[file:03-keyboard-and-mouse.org][Keyboard and mouse]] -- the knob again, with the keyboard alone and with the mouse: the header line's buttons, the menus, scrubbing slices and numbers, every action's key and gesture.
4. [[file:04-patterns.org][Patterns]] -- a fan grill and a honeycomb bowl: repeating a shape cheaply, rounding by offsetting.
5. [[file:05-imports.org][Splitting a design into files]] -- a pegboard hook that imports the board from a module.
6. [[file:06-text-and-svg.org][Text and SVG]] -- a name tag, and a badge from SVG files.
7. [[file:07-closed-forms.org][Shapes from formulas]] -- a vase whose outline changes with height, and an involute gear.
8. [[file:08-exporting.org][Exporting and printing]] -- bounds, resolution, and making the mesh as small as possible.
9. [[file:09-mx-keycap.org][An MX-style keycap]] -- a sculpted keycap: squircles, a stack of tilted slices, a dish, an even shell, a stem and a legend.

Each picture is made from the code above it when the site is built: the render comes from keycap's server, and the header line, completion, ElDoc and help come from keycap itself.
The Emacs around them is drawn in the colours of one configuration, so yours will look different.
The keys are the same.
